About the clinic

Personalized care for your recovery

Every patient's recovery journey is different. At Vijaya's Physio World, physiotherapy and rehabilitation are focused on understanding the individual's condition, improving movement, managing pain and supporting a safe return to everyday activities.

The clinic is led by Dr. Vijaya Pawar, with extensive clinical experience in physiotherapy and rehabilitation, serving patients from Goregaon West and the surrounding Mumbai suburbs.

Years of Clinical Experience

What we offer

Physiotherapy & rehabilitation services

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Structured physiotherapy support to help restore movement, strength and function following sports-related injuries.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Guided recovery physiotherapy to help rebuild strength and mobility safely after surgery.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy support for patients managing neurological conditions affecting movement and coordination.

Stroke Rehabilitation

Focused rehabilitation aimed at helping stroke patients regain function and independence over time.

Frozen Shoulder

Assessment and physiotherapy treatment aimed at easing stiffness and restoring shoulder mobility.

Osteoarthritis Care

Physiotherapy approaches to support joint function and manage discomfort associated with osteoarthritis.

Neck & Musculoskeletal Pain

Treatment focused on relieving neck and musculoskeletal pain and improving day-to-day movement.

Therapeutic Taping

Supportive taping techniques used alongside treatment to aid movement and recovery.

Mobility & Functional Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation focused on functional movement, balance and returning to everyday activity.
